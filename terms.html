What this is

Gimmick is a personal, self-hosted automation tool for YouTube. Each operator runs their own copy on their own computer and connects it to a channel they control. There is no hosted service, no account, and no fee.

Acceptable use

By running Gimmick, the operator agrees to:

  • Connect it only to a YouTube channel they own or are authorised to manage.
  • Comply with the YouTube Terms of Service, the YouTube API Services Terms of Service, and YouTube's Community Guidelines and spam policies.
  • Keep automated replies relevant and limited — the tool ships with conservative rate limits, and removing them to post at scale would be a violation of YouTube's spam policy.
  • Not use it to harass, impersonate, mislead, or post prohibited content.

Automated replies

Replies are generated by an AI model chosen and configured by the operator. Output can be wrong, unpredictable, or unintentionally inappropriate. The operator is fully responsible for everything posted from their channel, whether written by a person or generated by a model. Operators are encouraged to disclose that a bot is replying.

Gimmick relies on the YouTube Data API and on an AI provider selected by the operator. Those services have their own terms, quotas, and pricing, and the operator is responsible for their own usage and any charges incurred. Availability of those services is outside the control of this software.
